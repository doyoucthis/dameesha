About Me

Where circuits
meet creativity

I'm Dameesha, an electronics engineering student at Dawood University of Engineering & Technology in Karachi. I'm drawn to the elegance of signal processing, embedded systems, and the challenge of making hardware behave exactly as intended.

Outside the lab, I find that my love of art and creativity deeply informs the way I engineer — design thinking, visual problem-solving, and attention to detail come naturally when you see the world through both a technical and creative lens.

Currently in my undergraduate years, I'm building a foundation in core electronics while exploring how technology can be both functional and beautiful.
